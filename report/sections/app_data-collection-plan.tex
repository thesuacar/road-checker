
\section{Data Collection Strategy}
\label{app:data-collection-plan}

\textbf{Placement.}
The smartphone was placed in the participant's back pocket. This placement was
chosen to reduce direct leg displacement effects and to maintain a consistent
sensor position across participants.

\textbf{Initial collection plan.}
The initial target was to collect five recordings of each road condition per
participant, resulting in a planned total of 30 datasets. Each recording was
intended to have a duration of at least 30~s.

\textbf{Data anonymisation.}
Participants were assigned secret codes that were not associated with their
names in the final assignment material. Raw recordings followed the naming
convention

\begin{center}
\texttt{<Sample\_ID>\_<path\_condition>.zip}
\end{center}

For example:

\begin{center}
\texttt{Raw001\_Smooth.zip}
\end{center}

Participant names are not shown in order to preserve anonymisation.

\subsection{Additional Annotated Recordings}

On 20 September 2026, additional data were collected by participant~\#01
(\texttt{pt01}) to compensate for missing recordings and the acquisition issue
observed with participant~\#03. Two longer annotated recordings were added and
subsequently segmented into individual Smooth and Bumpy samples.

Because multiple segmented files originated from the same continuous source
recording, they were kept under a shared split-group identifier. This grouping
was later used during model partitioning and cross-validation to prevent
segments from the same original ride from appearing in both training and
validation subsets.
